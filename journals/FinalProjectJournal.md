My idea is for my project to be an anomaly game, inspired by Exit 8.

With the theme being "Stuck in a Loop", the first thing that came to my mind was anomaly games. I want it to be in a spooky hallway, you must make your way through each hallway but only when you see an anomaly, when you get it wrong, you are reset. If you see an anomaly, you go to the door at the end of the hallway, if you dont see one, you turn back.

Different hallways, whether it be paintings, positions of chairs, tables, different props, even maybe a different sound that plays.

I think that instead of turning back, I will just have two doors at the end of the hallway, one left and one right. Go left if you believe there was an anomaly in the hallway, go right if you believe there was not an anomaly in the hallway.

Planning on using procedural generation, I do not want it to all be hand placed because every attempt is the same. If the player memorizes it then there is no fun.
