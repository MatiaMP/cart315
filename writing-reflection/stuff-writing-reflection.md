300 Word Reflection - Matia Paki

In this chapter, we explore how game rules can completely change the way a game is played. By using your creative expression, you can make many different variations of a game based off of the base of it. Pippin demonstates this with his project PONGS, which is a series of variants on Atari's classic Pong. He shows us that simply altering a single line of code can change a game's meaning.

He tells us more about this in detail when using his project PONGS as an example. The base game, a simple game of Pong where we have 2 paddles and a ball. You are playing against an opponent, moving your own paddles and trying to make them miss to score a point. Within this, he has thought of how changing the rules could change the game. For example, Blind Pong is a variant where the ball and the divider are invisible. This makes it much more difficult, you lose part of your senses and must go off another. There is also Ghost Pong, another variation of the base game. In this instant, the collision detection is turned off between the ball and the paddles, so you must simply accept your fate, in which the ball goes through your paddle. 

The one that intrigued me the most is the Trace Pong variant. In this variation, the ball leaves behind a continuous white trail and transforms the screen into a web of strokes from paddle to paddle. It kind of becomes a painting canvas in which you and your opponent can use your imagination and play off of each other to create something, until someone finally misses the ball. This truly brings a different objective to the game, now you are not necessarily trying to score a point, but you could in some ways be on the same team as the other person in trying to paint all over the canvas (game area). These are just a few examples.

To conclude, Pippin's Pong Project is a great example on how rules can define a game, and making different variations can help explore new ways to think of game making. By treating rules as a flexible material for creative expression, designers can unlock new emotional states and discover new ways of thinking about games and play.
