This reminded me of the classic Pin Ball game, when the ball bounces on a bunch of different objects and goes in any direction. I am intrigued about using this as a function now.

Felt like I had to add an obstacle in the arena, taking inspiration from Pin Ball. Adding a circle in the middle just to add randomness.
